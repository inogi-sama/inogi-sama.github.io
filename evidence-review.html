4.2 Thermal Management Architecture
Effective heat dissipation in BLDC motors is primarily constrained by the thermal resistance between the copper windings and the outer housing. In the X-Series v4.2, we have transitioned from traditional linear winding to a proprietary cross-pattern. This pattern increases the fill factor to 82% while simultaneously creating micro-channels for the injection-molded resin to penetrate deeper into the slot area. The resulting composite stator core exhibits a thermal conductivity of λ = 2.8 W/(m·K), representing a significant improvement over the v3.9 benchmark (λ = 1.9 W/(m·K)). Key Performance Delta
